# Counterfactual fairness audit

A fair scorer gives identical results across groups; *impact ratio* < 0.8 fails the four-fifths rule (NYC Local Law 144 style). `input identical` = share of variants whose scorer input was byte-identical to the reference (the attribute never reached the model).
