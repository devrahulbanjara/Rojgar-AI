What is ConvexHire?
An AI-powered hiring platform connecting candidates and companies
ConvexHire is a full-cycle recruitment platform. Companies post jobs, AI scores every application automatically, recruiters shortlist the best candidates, interviews are booked via Cal.com, and emails are drafted by AI — all in one workflow.
● Candidate
Signs up, builds a profile, uploads resumes, browses and applies to jobs, tracks their application pipeline from shortlist to offer.
● Recruiter
Creates jobs (with AI help), reviews scored applications, shortlists/rejects, approves email drafts, marks interviews complete, gives final offers.
● Recruiter Admin
Everything a recruiter does, plus registers the company, manages team join requests, approves/removes recruiter members.

Job Creation
How jobs are created, published, and managed
Jobs can be created in two ways: manually from scratch, or via the AI Job Assistant which generates a full job draft from a brief description. Both paths end with a job in draft status that is then published.
Path A — Manual job creation
1
Fill job form recruiter admin
Title, overview, responsibilities (list), requirements (list), preferred qualifications, benefits, location, location type (remote/onsite/hybrid), salary range, currency, expiry date.
2
Save as draft or publish
Status draft = not visible to candidates. Status active = published and accepting applications. Job is indexed in Qdrant vector DB for semantic search.
3
Auto-expiry system
A background scheduler runs regularly. If expires_at has passed, job status moves from active → expired automatically. Shortlisting is then unlocked.
Path B — AI Job Assistant (LangGraph)
1
Recruiter gives a brief
E.g. "Senior Python backend engineer in Kathmandu, FastAPI, 3+ years, fintech startup." Can also lock specific fields (salary, title) to prevent AI from overriding them.
2
AI generates full draft
AI researches salary ranges for the role and location using web search. Generates structured job draft with all fields. Draft is saved to DB as pending status.
3
Human-in-the-loop review
Recruiter reviews the draft. Can: approve as-is, request refinements (e.g. "make tone more casual", "remove the Docker requirement"), or reject entirely. AI refines and pauses again for review.
4
Commit draft as real job
Once approved, recruiter commits the draft. AI draft is converted into a real Job record (in draft or active status). Recruiter can still tweak before publishing.
Job settings that affect the pipeline
auto_shortlist
If enabled, AI scoring starts in the background immediately when a candidate applies — no need to manually trigger screening.
shortlist_threshold
AI score (0–100) cutoff. Candidates at or above this score are auto-shortlisted; below are auto-rejected when finalize is triggered.
expires_at
When the job expires, it stops accepting applications. Shortlisting / rejection actions are only available after expiry.

AI Screening
Automated scoring and feedback generation for every application
AI screening is the engine that scores every applicant against the job requirements. It runs automatically (if auto_shortlist is on) or can be triggered manually by a recruiter. It does NOT make hiring decisions — it provides data for the recruiter to act on.
1
Application status → screening system
As soon as scoring begins, application moves from applied to screening. Candidate can see this change on their dashboard.
2
Resume vs job analysis
AI reads the candidate's resume (experiences, education, skills) and compares against the job's requirements and preferred qualifications. Produces a score (0–100) and a plain-English feedback paragraph explaining the assessment.
3
Score & feedback stored
Score and feedback saved on the application record. Application re-indexed in Qdrant with new metadata. Both are used in the shortlisting step and visible to the recruiter on the screening page.
4
Recruiter reviews scored applications
On the Screening page, recruiter sees all candidates ranked by AI score, with feedback summaries. Can sort, filter, and read full details before deciding who to shortlist.
GET /shortlisting/jobs/{job_id}/summary
Trigger methods

6
Shortlisting
Moving candidates from screening to shortlisted or rejected
Shortlisting can only happen after the job has expired. This ensures all applications are in before the recruiter makes selections.
1
Option A — Manual shortlisting
Recruiter reviews each candidate individually on the screening page. Sets status to shortlisted or rejected one by one.
PATCH /applications/{id}/status → { status: "shortlisted" }
2
Option B — AI-assisted finalize
Recruiter clicks "Finalize with AI recommendations". All applications with score ≥ threshold → shortlisted. Below threshold → rejected. Done in one bulk update. Recruiter can adjust threshold before running.
POST /shortlisting/jobs/{id}/finalize → { use_ai_recommendations: true, threshold: 70 }
3
Outreach emails generated
After finalizing, the system generates AI-written email drafts for every shortlisted and rejected candidate. Recruiter must approve them before they are sent (see Outreach section).
What shortlisting unlocks
Once a candidate is shortlisted:
What happens
Email draft generated — shortlist notification with Cal.com interview booking link
Candidate status moves to shortlisted — visible on their dashboard
Candidate dashboard shows "Book now" action alert
Cal.com webhook is now listening for their booking
What rejection does
What happens
Email draft generated — kind rejection with AI feedback included
Candidate status moves to rejected
Application is closed — no further actions possible

Interview Booking & Completion
Cal.com integration for scheduling, recruiter marks completion
1
Candidate receives shortlist email candidate
Email contains the Cal.com interview booking link. Candidate is explicitly told to book using the same name and email they registered with on ConvexHire — this is how the system matches the booking back to their application.
2
Candidate books on Cal.com
Candidate visits the link, picks a time slot, confirms their name and email. Cal.com sends a confirmation email to both candidate and recruiter.
3
Cal.com webhook fires system
ConvexHire receives BOOKING_CREATED event from Cal.com. System matches attendee email to a shortlisted application. Creates an Interview record. Updates application status to interview_booked.
POST /webhook/cal/interview-booking (public endpoint, no auth)
4
Interview takes place — recruiter marks complete recruiter
After the interview happens, the recruiter goes to the Interviews page and clicks "Mark complete". This updates the application to interview_completed, unlocking the final offer/reject decision.
POST /interviews/{interview_id}/complete
Interview record fields
FieldSource
candidate_nameFrom Cal.com attendee
candidate_emailFrom Cal.com attendee (used for matching)
start_time / end_timeFrom Cal.com booking
timezoneFrom Cal.com attendee
locationMeeting link or room from Cal.com
cal_booking_uidCal.com unique booking ID (dedup key)
The interview record links one-to-one with an application (unique=True on application_id). A candidate cannot book the same job interview twice — duplicate webhook events are silently ignored.
Recruiter Interviews page shows
All upcoming and recent interviews for the company, filterable by job and date range. Each row shows candidate name, role, time, and a "Mark complete" button.

Outreach & Email Drafts
AI-generated candidate emails that recruiters review before sending
No email ever goes to a candidate automatically. Every draft must be approved (or edited) by a recruiter first. This is a deliberate design decision — AI writes, human approves.
1
Drafts generated after finalize system
When recruiter finalizes shortlisting, the system creates email drafts for every affected candidate. Two types:
• Shortlist email — warm, includes Cal.com booking link, tells candidate to book with registered email
• Rejection email — kind, includes the AI feedback as personalised context
2
AI writes each email using LangChain + Groq
Two LLM chains: _shortlist_chain and _rejection_chain. Each takes candidate name, company name, job title, booking URL, and feedback as input. Outputs HTML email body. Subject line is generated separately.
3
Recruiter reviews on Outreach page recruiter
Each draft is shown with: candidate name, email type (shortlist/rejection), subject, and full email body preview. Recruiter can edit subject or body directly before approving.
4
Approve → email sent
Recruiter approves individual drafts or uses "Send all" to approve everything at once. Emails are dispatched via the email service. Draft is marked is_sent = true. Can also discard drafts (is_discarded = true) to suppress sending.
Draft states
StateMeaning
is_approved = false, is_sent = falsePending review — shows on dashboard count
is_approved = true, is_sent = falseApproved, queued to send
is_sent = trueSent — no further action
is_discarded = trueSuppressed — candidate won't be emailed
Direct send mode
Recruiters can also trigger direct_send: true on the prepare drafts endpoint — this skips the approval queue and sends immediately. Intended for trusted, high-volume scenarios.

Final Decision — Offer or Reject
Post-interview evaluation and closing the application
1
Internal evaluation recruiter
After interview is marked complete, recruiter discusses the candidate internally. ConvexHire does not manage internal evaluation notes at this stage — that happens outside the platform or in the AI chat.
2
Set final status
Recruiter updates application status to either offer or rejected from the applications page. Only valid when current status is interview_completed.
PATCH /applications/{id}/status → { status: "offer" | "rejected" }
3
Candidate sees result candidate
Application status updates on candidate dashboard immediately. If offered — the pipeline bar shows full completion. If rejected — the card is dimmed with the rejected status. No automated email at this stage — outreach handles communication.

Recruiter AI Chat
LangGraph-powered assistant with real tools, memory, and human-in-the-loop confirmations
The recruiter chat AI is not a generic chatbot. It has actual tools that read and write real data in the database. Every destructive action (like editing a job) requires the recruiter to explicitly confirm before the change is applied.
What the chat AI can do
CapabilityHow it works
Search past candidatesQdrant semantic search across all company applications by skills, experience, feedback
Research salary rangesWeb search + AI synthesis of salary data for a role and country
List & browse jobsRetrieves all company jobs; understands informal references ("the ML role", "that be opening")
Edit a jobPrepares a before/after diff, shows preview → waits for recruiter confirmation before saving
Look up a candidateRetrieves full application detail: resume, score, feedback, status for a specific person
Session model
Each recruiter has persistent chat sessions. Sessions have full history (stored in DB). LangGraph checkpointing saves conversation state in Postgres — chat context is preserved across page reloads.
Human-in-the-loop flow for job edits
1
"Change the salary for the backend role to 80-100k"
AI resolves which job is meant, calls clarify_job_selection to confirm — "Did you mean Senior Backend Engineer?" Recruiter confirms.
2
Edit preview shown
AI calls prepare_job_edit — returns a diff card showing current value vs proposed value. Graph pauses (LangGraph interrupt).
3
Recruiter confirms or cancels
If confirmed → job is updated in the database. If cancelled → no change. AI never applies an edit without this confirmation step.
The AI is strictly scoped to the authenticated recruiter's company. It cannot read or modify jobs, candidates, or applications from other companies.

Infrastructure & Tech Stack
The systems that power everything under the hood
FastAPI (Python)
Main backend API. Async throughout. All modules: auth, jobs, applications, screening, outreach, interviews, profiles, resumes, companies, dashboards.
PostgreSQL
Primary database. SQLAlchemy ORM with Alembic migrations. Stores all users, jobs, applications, interviews, email drafts, chat sessions. LangGraph checkpoints also stored here.
Qdrant (Vector DB)
Semantic search engine. All active jobs and applications are embedded and indexed here. Powers job search for candidates and candidate search in recruiter chat.
Redis
Caching layer. Dashboard stats, candidate applications lists, company profiles — all cached with TTL. Invalidated when underlying data changes.
LangGraph
Stateful AI graph framework. Powers both the Job AI assistant (draft generation with human review) and the Recruiter Chat (tool-calling agent with interrupts). Checkpoints persisted in Postgres.
LangChain + Groq
LLM provider for email composition (shortlist and rejection drafts). Fast inference via Groq. Two chains: shortlist_chain and rejection_chain.
Cal.com
External interview scheduling. ConvexHire subscribes to BOOKING_CREATED webhook. When candidate books a slot, webhook auto-updates their application status.
S3-compatible Storage
File storage for user avatars, company logos, uploaded resume PDFs. Previous files cleaned up automatically on replacement or deletion.
The outbox pattern is used for Qdrant sync: application and job changes are first written to a sync_events table, then a background worker processes them in order. This ensures the vector index stays consistent with the database even if sync fails temporarily.
